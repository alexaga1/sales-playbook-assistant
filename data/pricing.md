# Northwind Supply Co. — Pricing

## Plan Prices

Starter is free. The Pro plan costs $49 per user per month when billed annually, or $59 per user per month when billed monthly. Enterprise pricing is custom and based on team size and order volume; most Enterprise customers pay between $5,000 and $20,000 per year.

## Discounts

Annual billing saves roughly 17 percent versus monthly billing on Pro. Volume discounts start at 10 percent off list price for orders over $10,000 in a single quarter. Nonprofits and schools receive an additional 5 percent discount with verification.

## Contracts and Renewal

Pro plans are month-to-month or annual. Enterprise contracts run 12 months and renew automatically unless either party gives 30 days written notice before the renewal date. There are no setup fees on any plan.
