# Northwind Supply Co. — Sales Playbook

## Ideal Customer Profile

The best-fit customer is a company with 50 to 500 employees that orders office or warehouse supplies at least monthly and currently has no centralized ordering system. Companies already using a procurement platform are harder to win and usually compete on price alone.

## Discovery Questions

Good discovery questions include: who approves orders today, how do you track spend by department, what happens when someone orders outside the approved catalog, and what does a stockout cost you in a typical month. The answers reveal whether approval workflows and spend analytics will matter to the buyer.

## Handling the Price Objection

When a prospect says Pro is too expensive, do not discount first. Ask what they spend today on rush orders and stockouts, then compare that to the Pro plan at $49 per user per month. If they still push back, offer the annual billing discount, which saves roughly 17 percent, before discussing any custom pricing.

## When to Walk Away

Walk away from deals where the customer will not name a single point of contact or will not approve a catalog. Both are required for implementation, and deals without them are the ones that stall past 6 weeks and churn in the first year.
