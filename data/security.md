# Northwind Supply Co. — Security and Compliance

## Authentication

Northwind supports single sign-on through SAML 2.0 and OAuth, including Okta, Microsoft Entra ID, and Google Workspace. Multi-factor authentication is available on all plans and required on Enterprise. Passwords must be at least 12 characters.

## Data Protection

All data is encrypted in transit with TLS 1.2 or higher and at rest with AES-256. Northwind is SOC 2 Type II certified and completes an annual third-party audit. Customer data is hosted in the United States.

## Data Retention

Order history is retained for 7 years by default. Customers can request deletion of personal data at any time, and deletion is completed within 30 days. Backups are retained for 90 days and are encrypted with the same AES-256 standard.
