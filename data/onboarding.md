# Northwind Supply Co. — Onboarding and Implementation

## Implementation Timeline

A typical Enterprise implementation takes 4 to 6 weeks. Week 1 is kickoff and requirements: we document the customer's approval chains, budgets, and catalog needs. Weeks 2 to 3 cover data migration, importing the customer's existing product lists, users, and cost centers. Week 4 is training, delivered live over video in two sessions. Weeks 5 to 6 are a pilot with one department, then full go-live.

## What We Need From the Customer

We need a single point of contact, a spreadsheet of users and departments, and approval of the catalog before migration begins. Delays in catalog approval are the most common reason implementations slip past 6 weeks.

## Training and Support

Every plan includes help-center articles and email support. Pro and Enterprise add live chat and phone support during business hours, 8am to 8pm Eastern, Monday through Friday. Enterprise customers also get a quarterly business review with their account manager.
