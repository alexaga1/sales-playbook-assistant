# Northwind Supply Co. — Returns and Warranty

## Return Policy

Unused items in original packaging can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Opened consumables such as paper, ink, and toner cannot be returned once opened, except when defective. Return shipping is free on Enterprise plans and $9.95 per return on Starter and Pro.

## Defective Items

Defective items are replaced free of charge, including shipping both ways, on all plans. Report the defect within 14 days of delivery and include a photo. A replacement ships within 2 business days of approval.

## Warranty

Most equipment carries a 1-year manufacturer warranty. Enterprise customers can purchase an extended 3-year warranty at 10 percent of the item price. Warranty claims are handled through Northwind support, not the manufacturer directly.
