# Northwind Supply Co. — Product Overview

Northwind Supply Co. is a B2B supplier of office and warehouse equipment serving small and mid-size businesses across the United States. Customers order through the Northwind portal, by phone, or through a dedicated account rep.

## Plans

Northwind offers three plans. Starter is free and covers basic ordering for teams up to 5 people. Pro costs $49 per user per month and adds bulk ordering, approval workflows, and priority support. Enterprise is custom-priced and adds a dedicated account manager, custom catalogs, and volume discounts.

## Key Features

The portal includes real-time inventory tracking, order history and reporting, and spend analytics by department. Customers can set budgets per team, require manager approval on orders over a threshold they choose, and schedule recurring orders for consumables like paper and toner.
