# Northwind Supply Co. — Integrations

## Supported Integrations

Northwind integrates with Salesforce, QuickBooks, Slack, and Microsoft Teams. A public REST API is available on Pro and Enterprise plans for custom integrations. The API supports orders, inventory lookups, and spend reporting.

## Salesforce Integration

The Salesforce connector syncs orders and spend data every 15 minutes. It requires a Salesforce admin to approve the connected app once during setup. After setup, no ongoing maintenance is needed unless the customer's Salesforce permissions change.

## QuickBooks Integration

The QuickBooks connector posts approved orders as bills automatically. It supports both QuickBooks Online and QuickBooks Desktop. If a bill fails to post, the error appears in the integration status page with the QuickBooks error code.
